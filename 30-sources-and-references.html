30.3 Existing GS1 battery passport guideline ​

GS1 in Europe, Battery ID and link to DPP, issue 1.2, 23 December 2022. This position paper is based on proposal COM/2020/798 and predates the final Regulation. It is used for terminology and earlier GS1 positions only, and not as a source for current legal obligations, dates or scope.

30.4 GS1 Member Organisation publications ​

GS1 Netherlands, Voldoen aan de EU Battery Regulation met de QR Code powered by GS1, in Dutch, as a published white paper and a factsheet. These describe implementation patterns. They are neither GS1 standards nor sources of legal obligations.

30.5 Citation conventions ​

  • “The Regulation” means Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 as described in Section 30.1. A bare article, annex or recital number refers to it.
  • Legal citations take the form Article 13(6), Article 3(1), point (13), or Annex VI, Part A.
  • Cross-references to this guideline give the section number and, in running text, its heading.
  • Codes beginning OQ- identify the open questions in Section 29.6.
  • “Controlled source set” and “controlled sources” mean the documents listed in this section that have been reviewed for this guideline. An act listed as not integrated in Section 30.1 is outside that set.
  • GS1 standards are cited by name and the version shown in Section 30.2.
